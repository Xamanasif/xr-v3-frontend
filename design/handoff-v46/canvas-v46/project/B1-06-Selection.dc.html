Words
“Your selection”, “Continue to request”, “Remove”. Never cart, basket, checkout or buy. No prices (ND-08).
Formats
Only the experiment’s published formats can be chosen. The last chosen format is locked (“use Remove instead”).
Summary panel
Counts, formats chosen, one primary step (Continue to request), one way back to browsing. Below 1024 px it becomes a bottom bar.
Licences
Not asked here. The request asks for “Licences requested”; nothing on these pages says what a licence covers (D7 OPEN).
Persistence
The selection lives in the visitor’s browser session until the request is sent; it does not depend on an account. Whether requesters have accounts is OPEN (T-01 / R01).
