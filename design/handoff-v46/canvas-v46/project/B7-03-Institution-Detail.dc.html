Summary · Access plans (read-only) · Needs attention · Institution report (per format, never one combined figure) · People · History.
Assign Institute Admin
Name + work e-mail, validated. Nothing is sent from here — how they first sign in is open (T-11). Offered once access is active.
Pause / Restore (DL-2)
Reason required, kept in the history. Pausing shows “Your institution’s access is paused — your work is kept.” to its Institute Admin, Faculty and Students (board B7-04).
Status handling allowed
Pause (active or ending soon) · Restore (paused). Awaiting payment and ended institutions have no status action here: payment is confirmed on the request (Batch 1), and an ended plan comes back only through a renewal request.
Not here
No plan amendment (T-08), no licence arithmetic (T-06), no student feedback (T-25), no Create institution (T-09).
