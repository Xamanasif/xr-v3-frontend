Visual foundation

What we keep from B2, what we adjust and what we replace for readability. The product stays institutional, light-first and scientific: navy for structure, restrained gold for identity, controlled cyan for simulation cues, clean neutral surfaces.

Colour roles

Every colour has one job. Contrast is measured against white unless noted; text pairs meet 4.5 : 1, meaningful borders and focus rings meet 3 : 1.

Spacing scale

A 4-based scale. Use gaps between siblings, not margins on children. Generous, but controlled: overview sections sit 24 px apart, page header to content 24 px, form fields 20 px.
