Rules for GL-03

  • Same template, same place in every workspace: sidebar bottom and the account menu.
  • Fields: what happened, where (pre-filled from the page you came from), optional screenshot. Nothing else.
  • No response times, service levels or promises about who answers — the support model is open (ND-14).
  • When the list is empty, its primary action moves into the empty state.
  • The reporter reads the resolution note; resolving and reopening are Super Admin actions (SA-F8).
  • Status words: Open · Resolved · Reopened — identical to the Super Admin side.
