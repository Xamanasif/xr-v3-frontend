Unit of access
The experiment (LD-1). Step 1 selects experiments inside a course of an active access plan; the formats show read-only as “Available to your institution”. There is no WEB / AR / VR / MR picker anywhere in the flow.
Published ≠ held
Other published experiments of ME ZG523 are listed as “Not in your access plan” and cannot be ticked.
Who
Students (search, multi-select; “already has it” and inactive students can’t be ticked) or one group (empty groups can’t be chosen).
Review
Course, experiments, inherited formats, who, and the status each grant will get. The licence line repeats known data only (120 licences, term) — it never says what giving access does to the count (D7).
Result
Confirmation names each grant: “Rahul Verma → Shell-and-Tube Heat Exchanger → SACC-0310 → Active”.
Panel
Your selection ≈ 26 % on desktop; below 860 px of content it becomes a collapsible “Your selection” block under the step.
