Tabs
Questions · Drafts to review. Drafts are tagged “Draft — needs your review” and “Drafted with assistance”; they never enter the bank on their own (T-23).
Delete
The label “Delete” is settled; how it works underneath is open (ND-15). DL-1 says the question leaves the bank, its history is kept, and assessments that use it keep their copy.
Used in
Each question shows how many assessments use it. Editing a used question saves a new version; the assessment changes only when the Faculty chooses “Use updated version”.
Types
Multiple choice (one correct, up to four options) · true or false · written answer with free-text marking guidance (T-21: no rubric builder). No other types (T-22).
