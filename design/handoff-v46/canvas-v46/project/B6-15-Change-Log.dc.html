The publication check re-reads only stated prerequisites: Academic Reviewer approval, learning outcomes, catalogue details (prototype set, T-29), delivery details for each format. In the prototype chain these are already met upstream, so the blockers state is drawn with a sample (SUB-0070, outcomes missing). No other check is invented.
OD-B6-04When do the Vendor and the Content Manager see outcome edits?Prototype: at the decision — the set travels with Approve or Return. Edits in progress stay with the reviewer.
OD-B6-05References on a Content Manager send-back (OD-B5-10)Offered only when sending to the Vendor (they map to the Vendor’s editor); to the Academic Reviewer the reason only. References on update returns stay open (T-28).
