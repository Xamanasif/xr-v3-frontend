One mechanism
Give this group access opens the same Give students access flow (B2-05) with the group chosen — no separate group assignment engine.
Group grants
Shown for the members the group has when access is given (the current-members demonstration). Inactive members can’t open experiments while inactive. A member who already has the experiment individually is shown as “Has it individually”; what the group grant means for them, and what happens when one of their grants is withdrawn, is OD-B2-01.
Students added later
A student added after the group grant is shown as “Added after the group grant”. Whether they receive the group’s experiments is OD-B2-02 — open. The inventory row for IA-14 says “Group grants reach new members”; the Batch-2 review asked for this to stay open until it is confirmed, so no screen promises it either way.
Remove a member
DL-1 confirm. The dialog says only what is certain: they leave the group, their record stays. What removal does to access already given through the group, and to its history, is OPEN — not stated.
After Give this group access · SACC-0311Members show what the group grant means for each of them, in words.
