KeyboardEvery control is a native button, link, input, select or checkbox. Dialogs move focus to their first field and return it to the button that opened them; error summaries take focus; step changes move focus to the step heading.
FocusBatch-0 2 px focus ring; gold on navy.
FormsEvery field has a visible label; required / optional said in words; errors in words next to the field and in a summary.

MotionOnly the Batch-0 spinner and skeleton shimmer; both stop under reduced motion.
