Fix · 8 Oct 2026 · controls hidden under clipped frames

Reported
Hosted canvas, Play → Fill, 8 Oct 2026 (user test): on B4-14 the Student could answer questions 1–4 and saw “All answers saved”, but could not scroll on to Submit.
Cause
The attempt frame had a fixed height (1,250 px) and clipped its content (overflow: hidden) — the page inside is 1,720 px. A clipped frame cannot be scrolled with a trackpad, wheel or keyboard, so the written answer field and Submit sat under the clip. Local tests had passed because Playwright scrolls clipped boxes programmatically; a person cannot.
Same cause elsewhere
27 frames clipped a control: B4-02 (1), B4-04 (1), B4-07 (3), B4-11 (4), B4-12 (4), B4-14 (1), B4-15 (13). B4-13 and the other boards had none.
Fix
Every interactive frame’s height is now a minimum: the frame grows with its content (also after an error or a new row appears). The four B4-11 state frames that hid their actions grow too. The Submit dialog now opens at the foot of the attempt, next to Submit, instead of at the top of a tall frame; the Confirm marks dialog does the same where the marks panel sits under the answer (below 1,024 px).
Unchanged
Questions, wording, autosave, validation, dialogs’ content, the shared attempt record and every Batch-0…3 file. Frames whose content already fitted look exactly as before.
Knock-on
Boards whose frames grew are taller; B4-15 is now three columns so it stays under 8,000 px; the Batch-4 rows on the canvas were re-spaced.
New check
A “human reach” test: only mouse-wheel scrolling and mouse clicks at screen positions, and a control counts as reachable only if a hit-test at that point lands on it — run in a cross-origin iframe at native size and scaled to fill the width (iframe fixed to the board height, and iframe scrolling).

Policy safety check

RuleHow Batch 4 meets it
No defaults (C-5)Name, introduction, pass mark, attempts, deadline and release option start empty; no option pre-selected. 50% / 2 attempts / 14 Nov 2026 appear only as this assessment’s sample values.
Lifecycle (T-18)No “Make available”, open / close or “closed” control or state. Ready (rules + a question) = visible to students who have the experiment. The deadline is shown as a date; one assessment per experiment is flagged as open.
Release (T-19)No pre-selected release option; before release the student sees no figure of any kind — not even objective marks.
Re-marking (T-20)Confirmed marks are read-only. No re-mark control.
Rubric (T-21)Free-text marking guidance per written question only.
Timer and types (T-22)No timer, no countdown; three question types; never auto-submits.
Assistance (T-23)Drafts never enter the bank on their own; no provider named; no suggested marks.
Import (T-24)CSV + template; errors block, warnings don’t; all-or-nothing labelled interim.
Feedback (T-25)Format feedback is never shown to Faculty.
Reports and messages (T-33 · T-34)Export = CSV of the list on screen; no Reports page; no messages, reminders or notifications.
ProgressPer format in words and counts; no percentage, no experiment-level verdict (script check on every board). The only “%” is the pass mark the Faculty set.
AccessReach follows Student access (SACC); no format picker; no licence-counting copy.
No demo switchingOne person per frame; the Flow-4 board control sits outside every product frame and is labelled “not part of the product”.
