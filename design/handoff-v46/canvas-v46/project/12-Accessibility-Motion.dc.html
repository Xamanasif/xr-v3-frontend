Accessibility and motion

Built toward WCAG 2.2 AA from the foundation up: contrast measured, focus always visible, every control reachable and named by keyboard, status never colour-only, Help in one place, motion optional. The 1–5 scale below works.

Keyboard and focus

Every action works without a mouse. Focus is always visible (2.4.7, 2.4.11) and never hidden under the top bar or a menu.

Names and status · 4.1.2 · 1.4.1 · 4.1.3
  • Icon-only buttons carry a name: “Open menu”, “Close menu”, “Collapse menu”, “Remove filter Status: No access yet”.
  • The account button says who and which role: “Account menu: Meera Iyer, Institute Admin”.
  • Sidebar counts are spoken in words: “Marking, 12 written answers to mark”.
  • Status chips are text with a decorative icon; tones also differ in lightness and icon shape.
  • Current page, current tab and current step use aria-current plus a gold rule and bold weight.
  • Success and status notices use a polite live region; error summaries use role="alert".
Consistent help · 3.2.6

Help is the last sidebar item in all seven workspaces, in the drawer on phones, and in the account menu. The Help page is the same template everywhere (board 07).

Text and reflow · 1.4.4 · 1.4.10 · 1.4.12

Sizes in px tokens scale with browser zoom; every page reflows to 320 px without sideways scrolling — tables become stacked rows, section tabs scroll inside their own strip. Line height ≥ 1.4 for body text; no text in images.

Reduced motion · 2.3.3 · 2.2.2

When the system asks for reduced motion, the foundation stylesheet makes every animation and transition instant. Nothing in the product depends on motion to be understood.
