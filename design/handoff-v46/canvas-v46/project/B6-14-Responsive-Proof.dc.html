CheckHow Batch 6 meets it
Text sizeBody 16 px; hints and meta 14–15 px; format chips keep the Batch-0 code label next to words.
ContrastBatch-0 colour pairs only (≥ 4.5 : 1). Status is never colour alone: every chip has an icon and words; changed fields are marked in words (“Changed”, “Was / Now”, “Live now / Proposed”).
Keyboard and focusNative radios, inputs, textareas and buttons. Opening a dialog moves focus to its primary button (or the reason field); Cancel returns it to the control that opened it. After a decision, focus moves to the result. Moving an outcome keeps focus on the same control at its new place.
FormsEvery field has a visible label; errors are announced (role=alert) and repeated under the field (aria-invalid). Optional parts say “(optional)”.
TargetsButtons ≥ 40 px (outcome controls) and ≥ 44 px elsewhere — above the 24 × 24 px WCAG 2.2 AA minimum. Disabled moves are announced (aria-disabled), not hidden.
ReflowNo horizontal page scroll at 375 px; tables become labelled rows; the comparison stacks; nothing is clipped — every frame grows with its content.
Consistent helpHelp stays at the sidebar foot (WCAG 3.2.6).
