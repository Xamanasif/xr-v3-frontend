Status words, format chips and the state grammar

Status is always a word. Colour and icon shape support it; they never carry it alone. One chip family for WEB · AR · VR · MR. One set of patterns for every state a family can be in.

Status chips · tone, icon, words

Seven tones. Each tone has its own icon shape so it still reads in greyscale and for colour-blind users. The words come from the approved terminology (C.12) and stay identical as a record moves between roles.

Format chips · WEB · AR · VR · MR

One product-wide chip: same shape, size and type; each format told apart by its code and glyph — not by colour. The chip names a technology. It does not, on its own, say who has access or how far anyone got; the words beside it do.

1 · Normal · offered by the experiment
WEB AR VR MR

Tinted indigo. Catalogue, vendor editor, review. Means only “this technology exists for this experiment”.

2 · Available to you · inherited
WEB VR Available to you
WEB VR Inherited from the access plan

Navy fill (B2). Student: “Available to you”. Institute Admin giving access: read-only “Inherited” — no format picker (LD-1).

3 · Unavailable
AR Not included for your institution
MR Not offered for this experiment

One dashed treatment; the words say which kind of “not”. Never a disabled button in its place.

4 · Completed context
WEB Completed 12 Sep · feedback sent
VR Completed Give feedback on VR

The chip does not change when a format is completed. The status word says so; feedback is per format. No overall percentage.

Every message answers two things: what happened, and what you can do next. Plain words, no internal terms, no diagnostics for ordinary users.
