Interaction and accessibility

InputBehaviour
PointerSelect a neighbouring card to expand it. Previous / Next buttons move one area. Inside the selected card: Browse N experiments (catalogue filtered by the area) and View area (area page).
KeyboardTab reaches the selected card’s two links, then Previous / Next and View all areas. ← → move between areas and focus follows to the new card’s first link; Home / End jump to the first or last area. Neighbouring cards are not separate tab stops.
Touch (below 600 px)Horizontal swipe with snap; 16 % of the next card peeks so more is clearly available. Every card shows its full content and actions; the counter follows the swipe. Previous / Next are replaced by “Swipe for more areas”.
Screen readerRegion named “Engineering areas” (carousel); each card is a group named “2 of 9: Area name”. A polite live region announces the newly selected area. Images are decorative (alt="") because the area name is text.
Reduced motionNo size or scroll animation — changes are instant. Nothing ever moves on its own: no auto-advance, no timers.
ArtworkBespoke B2 artwork where it exists (three areas). Otherwise the pattern + initials fallback. Images never carry information.
DataAreas, descriptions and courses come from Super Admin Academic structure (SA-F6). Counts come from published experiments (CM-F3). The explorer is not hard-coded to three.
Long namesNames wrap to two lines in neighbours and in the selected card; nothing is truncated.
Track
Horizontal list, 16 px gap, scroll-snap to the start of each card, scrollbar hidden (controls and swipe replace it).
Card height
540 px at ≥ 1024 · 480 px tablet · 500 px phone. On phones the course list moves to the area page and the in-card “1 of 3” is replaced by the counter under the track, so text never sits on the artwork.
Selected card
Position (“1 of 3”), name, one-line description, its courses, counts, Browse N experiments (primary), View area.
Neighbour
Name and counts only; the whole card selects it.
Controls row
Counter · Previous · Next · View all areas (→ PU-02 All areas).
