Question
“What have we asked BITS for, and where is it?”
Two kinds
Renew ACC-0107 (mirrors the current plan — T-07) · More licences for experiments you hold (a new request). Both create a REQ that BITS decides in Super Admin › Requests — the same record, same status words as Batch 1.
Statuses
Under review · Information requested (reason shown, no reply — T-03) · Approved — payment required · Access active. No Declined state (T-02).
Licences
“Licences requested” as a number only. Nothing explains what a licence covers or how licences are counted (D7).
Not asked
A term for additional licences, new experiments or new formats (T-07 open). New experiments are requested through the public catalogue.

Form, detail and states

More licences · validationWhole number; the experiment comes from your access plan.
