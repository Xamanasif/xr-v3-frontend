Four separate events. Institution record (created at the first approval of a new institution) · request approval · payment confirmation (external only) · institutional access activation, which creates the access plan the Institute Admin will see (Batch 2). “Approved” never reads as “active”, and nothing on any screen says a student has access. Steps 07, 11 and 13 assume the requester has reached their request status: how they are identified and return is OPEN (R01) — the reference alone does not open it.
Internal route. If the Super Admin sets Internal at step 09, the confirmation says access becomes active now, the record moves straight to Access active, and the requester never sees steps 11–12 (board B1-08 shows REQ-0039 as the internal example).
