Layout (AR-4)
Main ~72 %: catalogue preview (only formats that passed review) → publication check → submission and review summary (with each format’s status) → history. Decision panel ~28 %.
Publish (DL-1)
Says once what publishing does: it appears in the public catalogue under its course with its formats, and it gives no one access. Result: Published.
Send back (DL-2)
Choose the Academic Reviewer (a re-check) or the vendor (revise and resubmit) and give a reason — both required. References are optional and shown only for the vendor (OD-B5-10).
Never here
No partial publish or format hold-back (T-26). No access, licence or Faculty control: publishing ≠ institution access ≠ Faculty mapping ≠ Student access.
