Layout (AR-4)
Main column ~72 %: re-check reason or what changed → learning outcomes → submission details (from the vendor) → formats and delivery → review history. Decision panel ~28 %, at the top of its column.
Learning outcomes
One list for the Experiment, every format (LD-3). Add, edit, move up or down, delete with Undo. The vendor and the Content Manager see the set you approve or return.
Approve (DL-1)
“Sends it to the Content Manager. Approving does not publish.” Needs at least one outcome (the “not ready” state).
Return to vendor (DL-2)
Reason required — the vendor sees it above their editor. “What it refers to” is optional; the vendor sees the same labels as links (OD-B5-10).
Not designed
No partial approve or per-format decision (T-26). No review checklist or rubric (T-21 / QD-08). No device testing (T-30).
