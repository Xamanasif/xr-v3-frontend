Signing in
Need access for your institution? Request institutional access
Pattern
AR-6 focused task: one centred card on the calm B2 brand background, BITS Pilani / WILP identity in the card. No navigation to wander off to — only Back to experiments and Request institutional access.
Two ways in
Sign in with your institution (single sign-on) and Sign in with e-mail. Which provider, and whether every institution has one, is OPEN (T-10).
Forgot password
XS-04 is hidden until activation and recovery are decided (T-11 / R02). Until then the link only says: contact your institution administrator.
Never here
DEMO strips, sample accounts, a role switcher, “Viewing as…”, or a “choose a workspace” step (multi-role is deferred).
After sign-in
Each person lands on their role’s home in the Batch-0 shell. Session expired returns them to the page they were on.
