What the figures mean

Students with access
Students who can open the experiment today. Each student counts once, however they were given it.
Started
Students who have opened this format at least once.
Completed
Students whose format was reported completed by the simulation or the headset.
Sessions
Times the format was opened in the browser (WEB) or a session was reported from a headset (VR).
Question
“How much is our content being used?”
Figures
Shell-and-Tube at Godavari, up to 24 Oct 2026 (sample): 5 students with access — Rahul (SACC-0310) and the active members of ME ZG523 — Sem II (SACC-0311); Arun is waiting for a licence and Sneha is inactive, so neither can open it today. WEB 4 started · 2 completed · 11 sessions; VR 2 · 1 · 4.
Same record
The same per-format progress the Student sees (B3-05). Rahul’s completed WEB and VR are inside these counts. Feedback is never shown here (T-25).
Not designed
Charts, licence usage or “in use” figures (D7), exports and a Reports page (R15 / T-33), percentages.
Open
Who in the institution may see Usage (A-12 / D18). The exact event that counts as “started” and as a “session” comes from the simulation (T-12).

Views and widths

By courseStarted / completed stay per format — never summed into a course verdict.
