Three short sections
Organisation and contact person · What you need (the selection, Licences requested, optional message) · For how long.
Never asked
Internal / external — BITS decides that at approval (T-04). Payment details or prices.
Requester identity
OPEN (T-01 / R01). The form asks only for institution and contact details and says why: “Provide your institution and contact details so BITS can review your request.” It neither promises nor requires an account. No OTP, magic link or registration step is designed.
Licences
“Licences requested” with neutral help text. Nothing says what one licence covers or how assigning students uses them (D7 OPEN).
Term
Monthly · Quarterly · Yearly · Until a fixed date (B2 term vocabulary). BITS confirms the term when it decides.
Validation
On Review request: an error summary (role alert, linked to fields) plus inline messages; fields keep what was typed.
Sent
Reference REQ-0042 identifies the request; it is never presented as a key to the request’s details. One primary action, Check request status, continues from this session. How a requester is identified and returns later is OPEN (R01), so the page promises no link, e-mail or account.
Same record
This exact request appears as REQ-0042 in the Super Admin’s Requests (board B1-10).
