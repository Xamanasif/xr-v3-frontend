Behaviour
  • Rail: icons with a tooltip on hover and keyboard focus; the accessible name is always the destination name.
  • Rail: identity (logo, product, workspace) returns when the menu is expanded.
  • Drawer: opens from the left, focus moves to “Close menu”, Escape or the scrim closes it, focus returns to “Menu”.
  • Drawer: choosing a destination closes it.
  • The expand / collapse choice is remembered per person.
  • The top bar always keeps the page title (or breadcrumbs) and the account menu.

Nested destinations · section tabs, not a second sidebar level

A sidebar destination that holds several lists keeps one sidebar entry. Its parts become section tabs under the page header — links that change the address, with the current part marked by the gold rule. The sidebar never grows a second level.

Navigation rules

  • At most 8 destinations plus Help.
  • Only real destinations: no disabled placeholders, no Settings, Reports, Content allocation or Vendor Updates.
  • Order follows §E and never changes by role state.
  • Current item: gold rule, light fill, bold, aria-current — never colour alone.
  • Counts only when they open the list they count; announced in words.
  • Help sits at the bottom, separated by a hairline, in every workspace (WCAG 3.2.6).
  • Workspace label in gold capitals; institution name below it for institution roles.
  • No demo strip, no “Viewing as”, no sample-account selector, no role switcher.
  • Detail screens, flow steps and tabs are reached from destinations — never added to the sidebar.
