Question
“Who can produce, review and publish content, and for what?”
Scope
All experiments · a course · one experiment. Scope decides what appears in their workspace: a Vendor’s course list, a Reviewer’s queue, a Content Manager’s library.
Without scope
Flagged on the list and the Overview; a submission whose course has no reviewer in scope gets stuck (see All content, SUB-0085).
Withdraw (DL-2) / Restore
Withdraw needs a reason, kept with the scope. Restore puts the same scope back (no reason asked — OD-B7-10).
Not here
No invitation e-mail, no SSO set-up, no new rule about where identities come from (T-11).
