Only you see the answer key and the marking guidance.

  1. Question 1 of 4 · Multiple choice2 marks

    What does the effectiveness (ε) of a heat exchanger compare?

    • CorrectThe actual heat transfer with the largest heat transfer possible
    •  The hot-side flow rate with the cold-side flow rate
    •  The overall heat transfer coefficient with the surface area
    •  The hot fluid’s inlet temperature with its outlet temperature
    Explanationε = q ÷ q_max, where q_max = C_min (T_h,in − T_c,in).
  2. Question 2 of 4 · Multiple choice2 marks

    How is the number of transfer units (NTU) defined?

    • CorrectUA ÷ C_min
    •  C_min ÷ C_max
    •  q ÷ q_max
    •  UA ÷ C_max
    ExplanationNTU = UA ÷ C_min — the exchanger’s size measured against the smaller heat-capacity rate.
  3. Question 3 of 4 · True or false2 marks

    Fouling on the tube surfaces lowers the overall heat transfer coefficient U.

    • CorrectTrue
    •  False
    ExplanationFouling adds a thermal resistance in series, so U falls.
  4. Question 4 of 4 · Written answer10 marks

    Explain how fouling changes the exchanger’s performance, using what you observed in the simulation.

    Marking guidanceLook for: fouling adds a thermal resistance; U falls; at the same flow rates the heat duty and the effectiveness fall; the outlet temperatures move towards their inlet values. Give credit for linking this to readings taken in the simulation, and for mentioning cleaning or a fouling allowance in design.
    ExplanationFouling adds a resistance, so U and the heat duty fall at the same flow rates: the hot fluid leaves hotter and the cold fluid leaves cooler.
