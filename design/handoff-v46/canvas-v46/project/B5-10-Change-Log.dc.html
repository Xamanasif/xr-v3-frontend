Policy safety check

RuleHow Batch 5 meets it
No Vendor updates (LD-4)No “Propose an update”, “Updates”, “Update detail” or “Add a format” anywhere (script check). Published experiments are read-only.
Approve ≠ Publish (LD-4)The Vendor never publishes. Submitting says “It does not publish anything”. Publish belongs to the Content Manager (Batch 6).
Neutral resubmit (M-3)After Resubmit: “Submitted — your changes have been submitted for review.” No gate is named in that state (script check).
One Experiment, its formats (LD-2)Formats are chosen inside one experiment; each reaches the catalogue only after review and publication. No per-format submissions.
One outcome set (LD-3)Learning outcomes are authored by the Academic Reviewer and read-only for the Vendor.
Packages (T-14)Fields labelled provisional; no source code; nothing uploaded — sample file names only.
Course mapping (T-29)Only assigned courses are offered; the field set is labelled a prototype set.
No fabricated XR evidenceNo headset screenshots, device integrations or 3D imagery; format chips and words only.
No demo switchingOne person per frame. The two board controls on B5-08 sit outside every product frame and are labelled “not part of the product”.
