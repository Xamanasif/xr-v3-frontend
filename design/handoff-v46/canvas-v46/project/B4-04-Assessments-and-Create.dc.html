Steps
Experiment → rules → questions → preview (AR-5, one step per screen) with a modest “This assessment” panel (~26 %) that becomes a collapsible block below 860 px of content.
Nothing pre-filled
Name, introduction, pass mark, attempts, deadline and when students see results all start empty. The 50% / 2 attempts / 14 Nov 2026, 23:59 values in later frames are sample data, never defaults (C-5).
When students see results
Immediately after marking · After the deadline · On a date (+ release date). No option is pre-selected (T-19).
Ready
An assessment is Ready when it has a name, its rules and at least one question; students who have the experiment can then see and start it. There is no separate “Make available” control (FA-13 · T-18 — open).
One per experiment
The prototype’s rule; shown as a refusal with a link to the existing assessment. Kept as an open decision.
Not designed
Timer, question types beyond the three (T-22), rubric builder (T-21).
