One row = one student × one experiment
The row says who can open what, how it was given (individually or through a group, with the grant ID) and its status in words. No anonymous allocation counts.
Formats
Shown read-only, inherited from the access plan. They are never chosen here.
Withdraw
Individual grants only: DL-2 with a required reason; work is kept; the grant stays in history (“Show withdrawn”). Group grants are changed on the group (B2-08). The dialog says nothing about licences — D7 OPEN.
Withdraw ≠ remove access
Withdrawing ends one grant. When it is the student’s only grant for the experiment (Rahul on 9 Oct), the dialog says so and that he can no longer open it. When the student is also in a group that has the experiment (Rahul on 12 Oct), the dialog names only the individual grant and says the group grant (SACC-0311) is not changed; the row stays visible as “Individual grant withdrawn” with the group line. Whether the student can still open the experiment through the group is OD-B2-01 — open; no screen states it either way.
Statuses
Active · Waiting for a licence · Student inactive · Withdrawn · Access ended (plan ended) · read-only while paused.

Try Withdraw… on his row: only SACC-0310 is withdrawn; the group grant is not changed. One older withdrawn grant (SACC-0312) is behind the toggle.
