What the refinement changed

Built from the reviewer’s work, not from the Super Admin dashboard.

One visual language across the role

The same type tags with distinct icons (New submission, Resubmitted, Re-check, Update) on the Overview, both lists and every item page. Course codes always with their names. Formats as one compact line instead of a tile per format.

Overview: a review desk

The oldest item leads with its waiting time and the only gold action. The rest follow oldest first with what to look at. Side cards open the matching queue tab, the queue filtered to a course, or a recent decision. Every figure counts existing records.

Lists you can scan

Both lists filter by type with tabs and counts; arrow keys move between tabs. The queue gives weight to what to look at and the waiting time; Reviewed by me to where each item is now. Loading rows match the table, and counts wait until the list has loaded.

Review pages: identity, weight, one decision

An identity card with a page map, then blocks in three weights: why it is here, your work, reference. The panel opens with Before you decide and ends with What happens next. The outcome rule shows before you try; return references are grouped by editor section.

Update: live against proposed

Why this correction, then live 1.2 against proposed 1.3 with changed fields only, then the live experiment. The panel and the confirmation both say 1.2 stays live until the Super Admin releases 1.3.

Internal IDs out of sight

Record IDs (SUB-…, UPD-…) are gone from every reviewer-facing label: the queue rows, Reviewed by me, the identity card and the breadcrumb, which now names the experiment. The IDs stay in the data, the routes and the search, so finding an item by its ID still works.

Decided items and refusals

A decided item keeps the review layout, read-only, with the decision in the panel. Records with few details no longer repeat course and formats, and a missing preview is said once. Each refusal is one card with its heading and two ways on.

Kept exactly

  • The rules. Approving does not publish, and publishing gives no one access. Approving an update does not release it: version 1.2 stays live until the Super Admin releases 1.3. At least one learning outcome to approve. Reviewers never see hold or withdrawal reasons, drafts withdrawn before sending, or courses outside their scope.
  • The content. Same records, statuses, filters and decision wording. No new figures, rankings, policies or features. Experiment names, course codes, vendor names and format labels stay visible; record IDs are now hidden from reviewer-facing labels and kept in the data.
  • The behaviour. Routes, the decision flow, validation, confirmation, keyboard use and every link from the completed role.
  • The foundation. XR-V3-RB-1.0 base files byte-identical; the role’s patterns live in rv.css only. The V1 Public Landing is untouched and RV-07 stays excluded.
