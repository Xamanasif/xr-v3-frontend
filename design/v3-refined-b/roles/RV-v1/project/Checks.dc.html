Where it ran

Headless Chromium 141 through Playwright 1.56, with the Claude Design type’s own runtime served as support.js. Public Sans was served from a local copy because this sandbox blocks Google Fonts, so line wrapping can differ slightly from the canvas. Each board ran at its own width; the journeys ran at 1440, 1024, 768 and 375.

Worth knowing

  • At 375 the New submissions tab label takes two lines; the tabs keep an equal height.
  • Standalone text links 19 to 22 px tall (breadcrumbs, All decisions, row titles) pass WCAG 2.5.8 through its spacing exception.
  • Full-page journey captures at 1440 and 1024 can show the rail where the window was scrolled. The board captures show it correctly.
