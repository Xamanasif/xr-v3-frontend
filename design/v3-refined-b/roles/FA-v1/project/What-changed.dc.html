What changed

One consistent Refined B design across all 21 Faculty IDs: the five R1 checkpoint screens carried over as they were, the R1 patterns applied to the other sixteen, the role styling consolidated into one stylesheet, and internal technical IDs taken out of ordinary user-facing labels. Per-screen detail is in the coverage register.

Internal technical IDs leave ordinary labels

Owner decision, 21 October 2026, extending owner decision 6 (Super Admin) to Faculty. Presentation only.

  • Out of every visible label, placeholder, dialog title, notice and accessible name: ASMT-, ATT-, Q- and D- identifiers. Records are named instead: the assessment or experiment by its name, the submission as “Attempt 1 of 2”, a question by its text, its type or its position.
  • Kept: academic course codes (ME ZG523) and institution-issued student IDs (GIT-2026-0418) — a roll number is not an internal technical id, and the CSV keeps its “student ID” column.
  • Kept out of sight, not removed: stable keys still drive routing, element ids, the preview’s record selector and the question search, so searching by an id still works.
  • No business rule changed: versions, attempts, one assessment per experiment, marking, release and import behave exactly as before — an imported batch still gets its ids, the finished notice simply no longer lists them.
  • Checked: all 219 boards swept for ASMT-, ATT-, Q- and D- in visible text, aria-label, title, placeholder, alt and screen-reader text — 0 found; and 204 interaction steps across the message, dialog and export flows carry the same guard.
  • One left, on a borrowed surface: Help & support still answers a sent report with “Your reference is SUP-0231”. XS-07 belongs to the shared XS contract, so it is flagged rather than changed here.

What stays exactly as it was

The redesign changes presentation only.

  • Functions and routes: every screen ID, tab, step, dialog and action is the one in the published build; in-role links are real anchors (no # placeholders).
  • Permissions: co-teachers see the same refusals; co-teachers are refused exactly as before and no answer key reaches a student view. Internal ids are hidden, never dropped: the same records, routes and keys are behind them.
  • Rules and states: three question types, CSV only, rules per assessment, versions on change after submissions, no re-marking, release policy unchanged.
  • Sample facts: the six sample worlds are unchanged; nothing was added to fill a layout.
  • Foundation: core.css, rb.css and rb-kit.css are byte-identical to the pack.
  • Shared XS surfaces: account menu, drawer, Help, Signed out, Not your workspace and the refusals are untouched.

Wording and layout changes a tester will notice

Same facts, shown differently. Journey checks were re-pointed for each, and the change is recorded in checks/specs/journey-changes.json.

  • FA-05 Mark opens the oldest waiting submission (FA-19), as on FA-03; it used to open the marking queue (FA-18).
  • FA-04 “Completed, by format” text lines became one unit meter per format with counts in words.
  • FA-06 the deadline moved from a sub-line into its own Deadline column, with days left.
  • FA-08 the submissions panel no longer repeats “2 of 5 students submitted”; it is a figure in the record band.
  • FA-12 waiting rows show “— of 16” with “Marked automatically: 4 of 6”; release shows “Not yet” with the rule beneath.
  • FA-14 a question’s sub-line is now a type tag, with “Version 2” when there is more than one version; drafts became review cards.
  • FA-17 the row check shows four figures (Rows, Ready, With errors, With warnings) instead of pill counts.
  • FA-21 the total reads “11 of 16 marks” with “Passed · pass mark 8”, as on FA-19.
  • Field hints shortened: “A whole number from 0 to 100.”, “For example 14 Nov 2026, 23:59”.
  • No-course states on FA-04, FA-06, FA-07 and FA-16 use the compact message card used elsewhere in the role.
  • Internal ids leave the labels: the assessment band and FA-06 rows drop ASMT-0009; submissions and the marking queue read “Attempt 1 of 2”; the bank’s quiet line reads “Version 2”; search is by “Words in the question”; the delete dialog asks “Delete this question?”; an export is named after the assessment.
