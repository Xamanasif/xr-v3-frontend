Design patterns used across the role

Defined in the R1 checkpoint and applied to every owned screen in the final pass.

P1
Priority band sized to its items, with the people or records behind each count; ivory only for urgent work
P2
Plan-tint identity band for a record (course, experiment, assessment) with fact figures
P3
Course ledger: tinted group header, column-aligned rows, no nested cards
P4
Unit meter beside counts in words, one per format; never a percentage
P5
Reading sheet at reading width with an action panel; the final action last with its note
P6
Sectioned authoring with a guidance and facts side panel; one primary in the footer
P7
Refined table: count, filters and actions in one toolbar; compact sub-lines; phone rows without repeated labels
P8
Sparse states: get-ready steps and compact message cards sized to their content
P9
Deadlines with days left and the release rule
P10
Records are identified by their name, the student’s name, the question’s text or its position — never by an internal technical id
