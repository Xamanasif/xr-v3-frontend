What is live here, and what is a handoff

  • Everything an Institute Admin does in this role is live inside this preview: adding people, importing, course codes, giving and withdrawing access, groups, faculty courses, requests and forwarding device requests. Returning to a list keeps what you changed.
  • Effects in other roles (what Rahul sees as a Student, the Super Admin’s decision on a request or device transfer, payment on the public request page) are documented handoffs. This canvas doesn’t change other roles’ canvases.
  • Help & support, the account menu and Signed out are borrowed from the shared (XS) contract with V3 tokens, pending the Shared Surfaces delivery.
  • Not designed, by decision: IA-12 Invitations, IA-21 Reports page, IA-22 Institution settings.
