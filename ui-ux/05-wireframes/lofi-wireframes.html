STU-01 · Home

The Continue Learning panel is the whole justification for Home: one deterministic target, one click to content. It exists only because D-07’s gating makes “the next thing” unambiguous. Needs Attention renders only when non-empty — a Student in good standing sees no block at all.

STU-07 · Simulation access — one component, four formats

Licensed XR modules are never hidden (D-09) and the desktop never shows a launch button it cannot honour (C-08). The VR panel states the D-07 consequence up front: a module that can only be completed on a headset will block the next module. That follows from D-07 ∧ D-09 and is not in the brief — a Student must not discover it at the gate.
