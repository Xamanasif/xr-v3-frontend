No cart — DA-01, DESIGN ASSUMPTION, REVERSIBLE. The source is explicit: "Whether checkout is single-item or basket-based is DESIGN-PENDING — no requirement specifies a cart model." Nowhere is a cart described as unnecessary or resolved.
No payment method is offered, because none is source-supported. A search across all three v5 FRD editions and every project register returns zero occurrences of payment method, mode, option, instrument, card, netbanking, UPI, wallet or bank transfer. FRD 9.1.1's acceptance criterion asks for exactly one thing — "a provider-neutral payment step" — and C-05 forbids inventing the gateway (Q-34, OPEN). An earlier draft offered three sample instrument labels; they were removed.

Absent by design: payment-method chooser · coupon · promo code · loyalty points · financing · instalments · tax line · invoice workflow · saved cards · billing address · gift · recurring toggle · term selector · renewal · cancellation. Nothing is ever collected — no card field, no CVV, no address, no phone.

Why one transaction updates six screens without any of them being told to.
