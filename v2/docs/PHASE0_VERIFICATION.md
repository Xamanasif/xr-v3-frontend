## 10 · Engineering notes

- **pnpm 12** uses `allowBuilds` for build-script approval and gates versions younger than its minimum release age; seven exact pins are listed under `minimumReleaseAgeExclude`. Install-time telemetry (`@scarf/scarf`) is refused.
- **Turborepo and Next.js** enable anonymous telemetry by default; nothing was opted in or out (`turbo telemetry disable`, `next telemetry disable` if the team prefers). `next dev` no longer writes `AGENTS.md` / `CLAUDE.md` (`agentRules: false`).
- **Testing Library** does not auto-clean between Vitest tests without globals; both jsdom setups register `cleanup()` explicitly.
- macOS Finder may drop `.DS_Store` files into `apps/` and `packages/`; they are git-ignored and the boundary tooling skips them.
- Local caches created by the run: pnpm store at `~/Library/pnpm/store`, Playwright Chromium headless shell at `~/Library/Caches/ms-playwright`. The scratch Node toolchain is session-temporary.
