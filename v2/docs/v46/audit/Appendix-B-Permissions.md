## How to read this

- **Evidence keys.** A board code such as `B2-04` means `design/handoff-v46/canvas-v46/project/B2-04-*.dc.html`; quoted text is the board caption, annotation, doc-kv or script string. `handoff/x.json` means `design/handoff-v46/canvas-v46/project/handoff/x.json`. `BC-n` = owner binding constraint n in `design/handoff-v46/START-HERE.md` (9 Oct 2026). `BR-nnn` = business rule in the Understand register (`wf/understand/decisions-rules.json`). `pe:<surface>#n` = `wf/understand/surface-<surface>.json` → `permissionEvidence[n]`. `STKxx`, `07.2-x`, `07.3 Rxx`, `PM-xx` = stakeholder / PM records under `ui-ux/00-project-context/` and `ui-ux/18-unified-functional-platform/stakeholder-review/`. `LD-x` / `M-x` = locked decisions in inventory v3. `T-xx`, `OD-Bx-xx` = open items in `handoff/open-decisions.json`; `REG-…` = Register v2 §T items carried in the Understand register (`registerExtras`).
- **Kind.** ALLOW = the design offers the action to the role. DENY = explicitly not permitted / not offered (designed absence, rule, or inferred absence). READ = visibility of a record or state. SYSTEM = a transition no human control performs (runtime, payment, release clock, workflow routing) — in the build a typed mock-service event, never a product control or board-style button. **OPEN** = an undecided capability: not drawn, or drawn inconsistently, with no designed treatment that settles it. An OPEN row is always UNRESOLVED and is **never offered in the build** (P4, deny by default); its Notes give the safe build treatment. Only ALLOW grants anything.
- **Status.**
  - **CONFIRMED** — stated by an owner binding constraint (BC), a stakeholder-confirmed record (STK), a locked decision (LD-x / M-x), or a handoff rule (entities.json business rules / owner-writer, navigation.json rules, B8-06, Batch-0 foundation board rules, CONFIRMED-DESIGN rules in the register, or an explicit ‘Rules’ statement on the governing board — basis BOARD-RULE) **and** the boards draw it consistently (or explicitly draw its absence), with no open T-/OD-/REG- item governing that exact point **and** no stakeholder record contradicting that exact point.
  - **PROVISIONAL** — drawn (control or designed absence) or a designed safe treatment, but not stated as a rule, governed by an open decision (T-/OD-/REG- id cited), or contradicted by a stakeholder record while v46 or a locked decision gives a designed treatment (build that treatment; the owner decides — §4.4). Basis `INFERRED` = the control is simply not drawn anywhere and no workflow needs it: never build it; the owner only confirms the absence.
  - **UNRESOLVED** — needed by a workflow but not drawn, contradictory between boards / JSON / stakeholder records without a designed treatment, or explicitly open with no safe treatment. Each carries the safe build treatment in Notes.
- **Decision ids on a CONFIRMED row** are related open items that do not change the permission itself (e.g. T-12 governs *which* runtime event marks Completed, not *who* sets it). Locked decisions (LD-x / M-x) are cited in Evidence, never as decision ids.
- **Basis** (why the status): BC · STK · LD-M · HANDOFF · BOARD-RULE · BOARD-DRAWN · SAFE-TREATMENT · INFERRED · CONFLICT · CONTRADICTION · GAP · OPEN.
- **Stakeholder conflict column.** A row whose own point a stakeholder record contradicts is never CONFIRMED: it is PROVISIONAL when v46 or a locked decision gives a designed treatment (build that), UNRESOLVED when nothing does. Flags marked ‘Possible’ are readings the owner should confirm. Two CONFIRMED rows still appear in §4.4 because the stakeholder point is adjacent rather than the row’s own: SUB-33 (the wider override targets asked for are SUB-35) and DTR-13 (the later record 07.2-C agrees with v46 and supersedes STK31-38).
- **Scope cells** on CONFIRMED rows may carry ‘PROVISIONAL: see …’ — the action is confirmed, but where the scope comes from is governed by the cited row.
- **Grid codes (§3).** C create · R read · U update/edit · T named transition/decision · D delete/remove · × at least one explicit not-permitted row · ? at least one UNRESOLVED row (including every OPEN row) · `·` no row. Letters are only counted from non-UNRESOLVED ALLOW/READ/SYSTEM rows.
