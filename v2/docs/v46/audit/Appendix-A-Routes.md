# Appendix A — Screen and route coverage (147 screen IDs) and App Router tree

> **Appendix A of the Phase-0 audit report** (`XR-v46-Phase0-Audit-Report.md`, Section 3). Source: `wf/final/routes.md`, final edition. An independent verifier raised 12 corrections against the draft; all 12 were applied (one in part), and they are logged at the end. The full 147-row coverage table is §1 and the proposed App Router tree is §3. The body below is unchanged; the notes here record how the rest of the audit reads it.
>
> **Reading notes added at assembly**
> 1. **Status words.** In the report's single legend, CONFIRMED and CONFIRMED (board-forced) both count as CONFIRMED. PROPOSED counts as PROVISIONAL: an implementer's choice, reversible, with no owner approval needed unless it is listed in §6. EXCLUDED means CONFIRMED as not designed.
> 2. **Surfaces.** The owner counts eight surfaces: Public plus the seven role workspaces. "Entry & shared" (XS-01…XS-08; 7 designed, 1 excluded) is not a ninth surface. Its screens render inside whichever surface the person is in.
> 3. **Session redirect (plan X-3).** The §3.2 tree and §3.8 row 1 name `src/proxy.ts`. In sample mode the session exists only in the tab's memory, so the Phase-0 plan uses a client `SessionGate` instead. `proxy.ts` becomes possible only with a cookie/BFF session (E-02). URLs are unchanged.
> 4. **Phase-0 interim landing.** Until a workspace home is built, `/<base>` redirects to `/<base>/help` (Appendix F §3.3, ADR-0005). This is an engineering-build interim, not a target route.
> 5. **Owner items here and their place in Section 9 of the report.** O-05 is the recommended interim for DV-5. O-06 (mock-identity exception) is part of gate G-2 (TE-1); the phase of XS-01 is gate G-8. O-10 is carried out in Phase 0 (WP-0.3). O-01, O-02, O-03, O-04, O-07, O-08, O-09, O-11 and O-12 are later-phase items in Appendix G.

**Status words used for each route decision (column "Status" in section 1)**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| CONFIRMED | `proposedRoute` kept. Only the notation is normalised: `·` lists are split into separate routes, `…` ranges are resolved to the drawn steps, `\|` enumerations are written as alternatives, the `[workspace]` placeholder is expanded to literal folders, and a default-tab parameter is left out of the canonical URL (rules T2 / T3) while the proposed form still resolves. Also used where the ID is a state, menu or action of a parent route, as screens.json says. A route that a row mentions for a product item with no screen ID (section 4.2: hub Assessment tab, IA request detail) does not change the row's status. |
| CONFIRMED (board-forced) | `proposedRoute` changed because a cited board frame makes the proposal impossible or wrong. |
| PROPOSED | The implementer's choice: the proposal is ambiguous, it conflicts with a board that does not dictate a URL, a URL the product needs is missing, or path routes are added beyond the `proposedRoute`. Needs no owner approval unless it is listed under owner decisions. |
| EXCLUDED | Not designed, by decision. No route, no link, no nav item. The `screens.json` disposition is quoted word for word, and any extra observation is given as a separate "Note". |

Query parameters marked "PROPOSED" inside a CONFIRMED row add linkable view state (filters, tabs, steps); they do not change the screen.

**Status words used for guards (section 3.8), defects (section 5) and states inside rows**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| CONFIRMED | Drawn or stated on a cited v46 board, or a handoff defect that was verified against the boards. |
| PROPOSED | The plan's choice for behaviour the boards do not draw. Needs no owner approval unless it is listed in section 6. |
| PROVISIONAL | The plan's interim reading of a board conflict, pending an owner confirmation listed in section 6. |
| UNRESOLVED | The boards conflict and the owner must decide. The plan states only an interim. |

Its own `not-found.tsx` then renders the board-08 workspace variant inside the shell. The `(hub)` group adds the header and tabs only to the five tab pages. `(hub)/simulation/page.tsx` and `simulation/web/page.tsx` share the folder name `simulation` in different groups. This is allowed because they resolve to different URLs.

### 3.4 Tabs: path segment vs query parameter

- **T1 path segment.** Tabs drawn as LINKS (nav + a[aria-current]) whose tabs have their own screen IDs, under a persistent record/section header, at a level with no dynamic sibling segment.
