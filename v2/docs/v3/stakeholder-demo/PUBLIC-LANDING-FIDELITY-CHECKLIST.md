## DEFECTs (none blocking; not fixed)

**DEFECT 1 — LOW: the skip link hides the top of the hero under the sticky header.**

- **Evidence:** Tab → Enter scrolls the page by 76 px (60 px at phone). `main` gets focus with its top at viewport 0, under the 76 px sticky header.
  - At 1440 the "Illustrative concept artwork · not a simulation capture" label ends up at viewport y 20, behind the header.
  - Image: `shots/product-1440-after-skip.png`; data in `a11y.json` (`skipResult`).
  - The skip link has no frozen counterpart; it is a product adapter.
- **Smallest fix:** in `landing-v1.css` add `.pl1-main { scroll-margin-top: 76px }`, with 60 px under the phone container query. Alternatively, have the skip target focus `main` with `preventScroll`.

**DEFECT 2 — LOW (outside `/`, console only): a new console warning on other public pages.**

- **Evidence:** on :3362, `/areas`, `/selection`, `/experiments` and `/experiments/<id>` log one extra warning, "`/_next/static/chunks/0opdzzm5ggtt8.css` was preloaded … but not used".
  - That file is the landing stylesheet. It is preloaded when Next prefetches `/` from the shell's home links (`scripts/warn2.mjs`, `scripts/warn3.mjs`).
  - :3360 does not log it. Pixels are unchanged (0 % diff).
- **Smallest fix:** accept it as framework prefetch behaviour and record it. Otherwise set `prefetch={false}` on the v46 shell's links to `/`, which is a small change to shared code.

## Observations (match the frozen file or an owner decision; for the record)

- **O-1:** The axe color-contrast hits at ≥ 768 come from the frozen entry dim of the story index row (`ixo = .25 + …`, 1347).
  - The row is still below the fold while it is dim, and it is fully opaque once it is visible.
  - An automated gate that scans the whole page on load will flag it. The lead may want to pin the stage before scanning, or record an exception.
- **O-2:** Focus rings use `:focus-visible` (D-10). The frozen `:focus` ring that appears after a mouse click (`sbs-1440-story-3.png`, `diff-1440-hero-pick3.png`) is intentionally absent.
- **O-3:** The frozen harness does not visibly play the intro stagger. Its elements are at opacity 1 when first rendered, though the transitions are declared.
  - The product plays the stagger as coded. Before hydration, the server-rendered hero copy is at opacity 0 for about 0.5 s.
- **O-4:** The "Request institutional access" button uses the closing band's navy 56 px button style, not an outline style. The owner wrote "frozen secondary-button style"; confirm that this reading is the intended one.
- **Also noted:** Pause keeps the frozen semantics: its label changes ("Pause motion" / "Play motion") and it also sets `aria-pressed=true` while motion runs (2011–2012). This is identical to the frozen file, but the combined label change and pressed state may read oddly in a screen reader.
