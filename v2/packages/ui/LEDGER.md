WP-0.6 (Appendix F WP-0.6). `src/ledger.test.ts` reads Appendix E §3 and fails unless the tables
below list exactly its 42 Phase-0 rows (implemented), its 14 later-phase rows (deferred) and its 2
not-built rows; each implemented row's unit test must cite every board and frame named here, its
browser spec must exist and every class named here must be defined by the v46 stylesheets or the
product layer.

**implemented (provisional: …)** — built, but an owner decision it depends on is still open and is
named (it is not resolved by the component).
